Platform Subscriptions & API Keys

IntakeOS runs on 8 platforms. All accounts are created by your clinic, paid directly to each vendor, in your clinic's name. Arbitrage Claw never holds your credentials or pays your platform bills. Our team handles all technical configuration once your keys are provided.

Why these specific platforms?

Every platform on this list was selected because it has a documented, tested API that IntakeOS has already been built and verified against. Substituting any platform creates a custom build — which changes the pricing, timeline, and warranty on your deployment. The stack is not arbitrary — it is how we guarantee the 15-second response time and compliance logging we promised.

💡 How to get your API key: After subscribing to each platform, navigate to their account settings or developer/API section. Copy your API key and paste it in the field provided. Your keys are transmitted securely and never stored by Arbitrage Claw — they go directly to your system's engineer for configuration.

Your clinic owns all patient data collected through IntakeOS. Upon termination of service, all data will be exported and delivered to you within 14 business days, then permanently deleted from our systems.
