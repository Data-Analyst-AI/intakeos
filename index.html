Inbound calls ring your staff first. If no one answers after 3 rings — because your team is already speaking with other patients — IntakeOS takes over instantly. No voicemail. No lost lead. No callback chase.

IntakeOS qualifies and books — without staff involvement

Our AI conducts a natural qualifying conversation, answers financing questions, and books the consultation directly into the doctor's calendar. Your staff receive a Pre-Consultation Brief before the patient arrives — procedure interest, financing questions, and key concerns already documented.

IntakeOS sends reminders at 48 hours and on the day of the appointment. If a patient doesn't show, an automated recovery workflow fires immediately — a warm follow-up text and call to rebook.

Compliance

Built for regulated
healthcare environments.

Patient data in healthcare is not optional compliance — it is a legal obligation. IntakeOS was architected for it from day one.

🇺🇸

United States — HIPAA

All US clinic deployments operate on HIPAA-compliant hosting infrastructure. Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) are signed with every applicable platform vendor before any patient data is processed. No patient data is stored on shared or non-compliant infrastructure.

🔒 HIPAA Compliant
🇨🇦

Canada — PIPA & PIPEDA

Canadian clinic deployments run on a private server physically located in Montreal, Canada. Patient data never leaves Canadian soil. This satisfies BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's federal PIPEDA standard. The clinic is the data custodian — IntakeOS never has access to patient data.

🔒 PIPA & PIPEDA Compliant
📋

NemoClaw Audit Logging

Every patient interaction, every automated message, every booking confirmation is logged in an append-only compliance audit trail by NemoClaw — the security layer running on the clinic's own server. Compliance reports can be generated on any interval required. The data belongs entirely to the clinic.

🔒 Append-Only Audit Trail
🔐

Infrastructure Security

All data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3 encryption. Network egress is restricted to a whitelist of 6 approved API endpoints — no unauthorized outbound connections are possible. The clinic owns its own server. There is no shared infrastructure and no vendor lock-in.
