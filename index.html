How IntakeOS works

01

A potential patient reaches out

IntakeOS provides immediate patient intake across three scenarios, ensuring inquiries are handled without waiting for staff availability.

🌙

Evenings & Weekends

When your clinic is closed, IntakeOS handles every inquiry without staff involvement.

🌐

Web Form Inquiries — 24/7

Web form submissions are designed to receive a personalized response in under 15 seconds, around the cloc

📞

Overflow Trigger — After 3 Rings

Inbound calls ring your staff first. If no one answers after 3 rings — because your team is already speaking with other patients — IntakeOS takes over instantly. No voicemail delay. No unnecessary callback chase.

02

IntakeOS qualifies and books — without staff involvement

IntakeOS conducts a natural qualifying conversation using your practice's own criteria, answers financing questions, and books the consultation directly into the doctor's calendar. A confirmation is sent by SMS and email. Your staff receive a Pre-Consultation Brief before the patient arrives — procedure interest, financing questions, and key concerns already documented.

03

Reminders, no-show recovery — then you meet your patient

IntakeOS sends reminders at 48 hours and on the day of the appointment. If a patient does not attend, a recovery workflow follows up and offers to rebook.

✓

The human moment: IntakeOS manages every step from inquiry to booked consultation. The face-to-face consultation with your clinical team is 100% human. You are charged $150 USD only when a qualified patient attends their scheduled appointment.

Compliance

healthcare environments.

Patient data in healthcare is a legal obligation, not an afterthought. IntakeOS is designed with compliance requirements built into the deployment architecture from the start.

🇺🇸

United States — HIPAA

US clinic deployments are designed for HIPAA-compliant infrastructure. US clinic deployments are designed for HIPAA-compliant infrastructure. Production environments, Business Associate Agreements, access controls, data handling, and infrastructure requirements are verified before any patient information is processed.

🔒 Designed for HIPAA-Compliant Deployment
🇨🇦

Canada — PIPA & PIPEDA

Canadian clinic deployments are designed around BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's federal PIPEDA standard. Canadian deployments use a private server physically located in Canada. Data residency and access controls are verified before patient information is processed. The clinic is the data custodian.

🔒 Designed for Canadian Privacy Requirements
📋

Full Audit Trail

Every patient interaction, automated message, and booking confirmation is logged in an append-only audit trail, powered by NemoClaw, running on the clinic's own server. Compliance reports can be generated on any interval required. The data belongs entirely to the clinic.

🔒 Append-Only Audit Logging
🔐

Infrastructure Security

All data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3 encryption. Network egress is restricted to a defined set of approved API endpoints. The clinic owns its server. There is no shared infrastructure and no vendor lock-in.
