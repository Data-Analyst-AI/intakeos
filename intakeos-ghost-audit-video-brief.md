## Objective

Show a non-technical audience exactly what happens to a patient inquiry **without** IntakeOS, and exactly what happens **with** IntakeOS. No actors required. No stock footage of smiling doctors. The data and the words carry the video.

Aesthetic: dark background, clean text on screen, phone mockup screen recordings, real Retell AI audio for the AI voice scenes.

**Visual:** Phone screen mockup. 7pm timestamp visible.

A patient is on their phone searching Google for "LASIK surgery near me." They find a clinic, tap the number. It rings. 4 rings. Voicemail. The patient sighs. They open Google again and scroll to the next result.

**Text on screen (staggered, dark background):**

> "It's been 47 hours."

**Visual:** Same scenario. Phone screen mockup. 7pm timestamp. Patient taps the same clinic number.

**AI voice (Retell AI audio, natural-sounding):**

Patient: "I'm interested in LASIK surgery."

Alex: "Perfect. I have a few quick questions to make sure we can help you. Do you currently wear glasses or contact lenses?"

Patient answers. Alex asks one or two more qualifying questions.

Alex: "Great — you sound like an excellent candidate. I have availability Tuesday at 10am or Thursday at 2pm. Which works better for you?"

Patient: "Tuesday works."

Alex: "Done — you're booked for Tuesday at 10am. You'll receive a confirmation by text and email in the next few minutes."
